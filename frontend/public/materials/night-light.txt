PROJECT: NIGHT LIGHT

MATERIALS
- Arduino Uno + USB cable
- 1 × Light sensor (LDR / photoresistor)
- 1 × LED (5 mm, white or yellow works well)
- 1 × 10 kΩ resistor (for the light sensor)
- 1 × 220 Ω resistor (for the LED)
- Breadboard
- 6 × jumper wires (male-to-male)

ALSO NEEDED
- A computer with the free Arduino IDE installed
- Optional: 9 V battery + snap connector, so it runs without the computer
