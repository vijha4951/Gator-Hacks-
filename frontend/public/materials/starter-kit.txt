PROJECT: STARTER KIT

MATERIALS
- Arduino Uno + USB cable
- Breadboard (830 tie points)
- Jumper wires, assorted (male-to-male and male-to-female)
- LEDs, assorted colors (red, green, blue, white)
- Resistor assortment (220 Ω, 330 Ω, 1 kΩ, 10 kΩ)
- Pushbuttons
- Light sensor (LDR / photoresistor)
- Potentiometer (10 kΩ)
- Piezo buzzer
- 9 V battery + snap connector

NICE TO HAVE
- Digital multimeter
- Small storage box for parts
